Connect your Square, or bring a CSV.

Regulars reads your sales history, works out what each customer actually does, and writes them the one message that fits. Not a blast. One message per person, based on their own orders.

Your business

Every message is signed with your business name, and its legal footer carries who you are. Australia: Spam Act 2003 — sender identity including your ABN, and a working unsubscribe. United States: CAN-SPAM — a valid physical postal address in every commercial email, and opt-outs honoured within 10 business days. The market also sets season references and spelling.

Applies to every message immediately. Nothing is sent anywhere by this app.

Two ways in

Connect Square

Read-only. Orders, Customers and Catalogue permissions, nothing else. Revocable any time from your Square Dashboard. In production this is Square’s OAuth screen; here it is a button.

Or upload exports

Prefer not to connect anything? Upload what you already have. Two shapes are accepted: your Square dashboard exports (transactions plus, optionally, the customer directory), or our simple one-row-per-visit format. Column names are matched tolerantly and you’ll get a report of what was found and what wasn’t.

Up to 50 MB in total. A bigger export? Pick a shorter date range in Square: the last two years is plenty.
These contacts opted in at my POS, on my website or through my loyalty program. I’m confirming that as the venue — exports don’t carry consent, and under the Spam Act a card payment is not consent.

Then three questions

  1. What do you want more of?Specific times you want busier, and specific products you want to sell. “Tuesday and Wednesday dinner” and “espresso martinis” beat “more revenue” every time, because the message can name the thing.
  2. What will you give, and to whom?Offers live in categories — an upgrade, something free, money off, a group perk — and each rule picks one. You can change every pairing. There is no blanket discount, because a blanket discount is wrong for most of your customers and expensive for the rest.
  3. How often is too often?Default: at most one message per person every 14 days, and nobody who’s been in this week gets anything at all.
