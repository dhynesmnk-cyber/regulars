Why this order

  1. Birthday always comes firstIt’s time-critical. A birthday inside the window beats a coffee nudge, whatever your priority setting says.
  2. Then whatever you said you wanted most ofYour priority is worth a nudge up the order for the rules that serve it. It doesn’t delete the others.
  3. Then most specific to least“You order the flat white constantly” is a stronger message than “we miss you”, so it gets used while it’s true.
  4. Upsells only go to people currently coming inPitching a free side to someone who hasn’t visited in months is tone-deaf and wasted. They get a win-back instead.
  5. Win-backs are for real lapsesA nudge about their usual is for someone running late. Past your lapse threshold it becomes a credit, because a nudge won’t bring them back.
